# Synopsis - Paro Wars

Eight nations of Paro World, 7 chapters a side (`DEMO/TXT_<NATION><n>`, 0 to 6). The player picks a nation; each campaign has its own chapter texts, and the plot shape repeats.

**Nations:** Pentaro Kingdom (`PEN`, penguins, King Pentaro III), Lovely Federal States (`LOV`, Hikaru), Bow-Wow (`BAU`, dogs, Prime Minister Pochi-no-Arashi), Republic of Akashiya Land (`TAK`, octopus Fuehrer A. Takosuke Jr.), Koitsu & Aitsu (`AIT`/`KOI`, General Secretary Koitsu speaks Greek, President Aitsu), Moai Islands Emirate (`MOA`, Empress Yoshiko VII), Panda Tea Party Beasts' Republic (`PAN`, Anna Pavlova), Michael Empire (`BUT`, pig emperor Michael).
**Blocs:** Penta Alliance (the West, led by Pentaro Kingdom), Aladio Alliance, Koitsu & Aitsu Pact (the East).

**Each chapter:** a nation is drawn into a petty war by bad advice or a shady deal, wins, then is pulled into the next. Behind nearly every incident stands John Nyanjiro, the mysterious peddler cat of the seven seas, who sells tips, loans and his friend Tenpe's feng-shui readings to everybody. Tenpe's readings always end in "you must have that land". Examples: February 7th Island (Koitsu & Aitsu vs Bow-Wow), Prince Kingdom's gold, the Gwait region's oil, oil embargoes. Nyanjiro is a beloved, trusted comic figure until the last chapter: **don't hint at his villainy before chapter 6.**

**Chapter 6, ending (`ENDING/<NATION>_TXT`):** the winner holds all 8 national treasures and plans a Peace Memorial Statue, on land Nyanjiro kindly offers. At the handover the treasures trigger his plan and he is revealed. **Late reveal:** he is Nya-Mu, Emperor of the Nya Empire, which sank 12,000 years ago; he was frozen in ice by the same disaster. The national treasures are his old daily things, scattered by the eruption and handed down as sacred relics:
Holy Grail = cat food bowl, Divine Fang = can opener, Fairy Sword = knife, Piccolo Mirror = spoon, Colt Lancer = fork, Divine Box = claw-sharpening stone, Urn of Immortality = catnip, Divine Orb = ball to play with.
He stirred up every war to get them back, all for his meal and playtime. The heroes ask why they were treasures at all: "We just kept handing them down because it's tradition, it seems."

`ENDING/TXT_END` is the shared epilogue and the treasure list. Credits are English.
