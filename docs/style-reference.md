# Style reference - Paro Wars

Comedy war parody. Punchy, silly, mock-serious narration; every leader is a cartoon animal with a speech tic. Short sentences. Keep every joke.

## Pairs
- 「そいつは勿体ないニャ〜」 -> "Such a waste, letting good land sit idle, nyaa."
- 戦闘開始だニャ～ン -> "Let the battle begin, nyaaan!"
- ニャめんなよ～！ -> "Don't nyou dare mock me!"
- ワシラの恐さを見せちゃる！ -> "We'll show ya what fear is!"
- Corrected: `２月７日島` once came out "that island", which lost the running joke of the date: always "February 7th Island".
- Corrected: `よしこ女帝` once came out "Yoshiko Empress": always "Empress Yoshiko".

## Shouts
Every character shares some stock lines (`またいつでも相手してやるぜ`, `あたれー`): the fill reuses one English; override it in `.part` when the character's voice needs it.
Bursts, not sentences. `Eat this!!`, `Th-they got meee!`, `What?! Wiped out?!`. Stretch vowels for ー and ～. Two lines of 168 px hold about 60 letters, usually fewer is funnier.

## Menus
Plain imperative or noun: `Attack`, `Move`, `Next page`, `Back`. Title case for single labels, sentence case for messages. Comic messages stay comic: `墜落で～す。` -> "Crash-laaanding!".
